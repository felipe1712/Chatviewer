ISO/IEC 27037 PRINCIPLES

How We Sustain Courtroom Admissibility

Evidentiary admissibility remains the sole prerogative of the court. The expert's duty is to demonstrate that evidence has suffered no alteration, that methods are fully repeatable, and that the chain of custody is unbroken.

Dual Cryptographic Hashing: SHA-256 & MD5

Simultaneously calculates both cryptographic hash algorithms over the evidentiary source at ingestion and over every exported artifact, eliminating collision ambiguity.

Cryptographically Chained Audit Trail (Hash Chaining)

Every forensic action (ingestion, decoding, reconciliation, export) creates a cryptographic block embedding the prior event's hash, making retrospective alterations mathematically impossible.

Verifiable Control Totals & Reconciliation

Rigorous evidentiary ledger: Messages in source = Messages parsed = Messages exported in PDF/RSMF. Any corrupted or discarded record is documented in the audit appendix.

RAM-Only Decryption Key Isolation

Symmetric AES keys for opening encrypted WhatsApp backups are held strictly in volatile system RAM and purged immediately upon process exit, never leaving a trace on persistent storage.
